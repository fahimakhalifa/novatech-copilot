# Community Tips (User-Submitted)

These tips were submitted by NovaTech customers on our community forum. They are not reviewed by NovaTech.

## Getting Better Battery Life on the NovaPhone X
Turning on adaptive refresh rate and dark mode noticeably improves battery life. Several users also recommend disabling background app refresh for social media apps.

## Pairing SoundWave Headphones With Two Devices
Hold the power button for 5 seconds until the LED flashes blue, then pair the second device from its Bluetooth settings.

## Cleaning the NovaBook Keyboard
Use compressed air at an angle and a slightly damp microfibre cloth. Never spray liquid directly on the keyboard.
