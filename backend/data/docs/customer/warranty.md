# Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty
All NovaTech-branded products come with a 2-year limited warranty from the date of delivery.
Accessories (cables, cases, chargers) have a 1-year warranty.
Third-party brand products sold by NovaTech carry the manufacturer's warranty.

## What Is Covered
The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use, including battery capacity dropping below 80% within the first 12 months.

## What Is Not Covered
- Accidental damage such as drops, cracked screens, or liquid damage
- Damage from unauthorised repairs or modifications
- Normal wear and tear, such as scratches on the casing
- Loss or theft

## NovaCare Extended Protection
NovaCare extends the warranty to 3 years and adds cover for accidental damage (up to 2 claims per year, with a EUR 49 service fee per claim).
NovaCare must be purchased within 60 days of the original product purchase.

## Making a Claim
Customers submit warranty claims through Customer Support with their order number and a description of the fault.
Repairs are usually completed within 10 business days. If a repair is not possible, the product is replaced with the same or an equivalent model.
