# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery Areas
NovaTech ships to the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, France, and Luxembourg. We do not currently ship outside these countries.

## Shipping Options and Costs
- Standard delivery (2 to 4 business days): EUR 4.95, free for orders over EUR 50.
- Express delivery (next business day, order before 20:00): EUR 9.95.
- NovaPlus members get free express delivery on all orders.

## Order Processing
Orders placed before 20:00 on a business day are processed the same day. Orders placed on weekends are processed the next Monday.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed as soon as an order ships. Customers can also see the order status under "My Orders".
Order statuses are: processing, shipped, delivered, cancelled, and returned.

## Missing or Late Deliveries
If a standard delivery has not arrived 7 business days after shipping, customers should contact Customer Support. We will open an investigation with the carrier and send a replacement or refund if the parcel is confirmed lost.
