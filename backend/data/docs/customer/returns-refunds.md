# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return Window
Most products can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, no reason needed.
Opened software, gift cards, and personalised (engraved) products cannot be returned.
During the holiday period, items delivered between 15 November and 24 December can be returned until 15 January of the following year.

## Condition of Returned Items
Items must be returned with all original accessories, manuals, and packaging.
Items with physical damage caused by the customer are not eligible for a full refund; a partial refund of up to 50% may be offered after inspection.

## How to Start a Return
Customers start a return from "My Orders" on the NovaTech website or by contacting Customer Support with their order number.
A prepaid return label is emailed within 24 hours.
Returns are free for NovaPlus members. For other customers, a EUR 5.95 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund, unless the item is faulty.

## Refund Timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse.
Bank processing can add a further 3 to 7 business days.

## Faulty or Damaged on Arrival
If an item arrives damaged or faulty, customers must report it within 7 days of delivery. NovaTech covers all return shipping costs and will send a replacement or issue a full refund, at the customer's choice.

## Cancelled Orders
Orders can be cancelled free of charge while their status is "processing". Once an order is "shipped", it must be returned instead.
