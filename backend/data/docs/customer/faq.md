# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is NovaPlus?
NovaPlus is NovaTech's membership programme. It costs EUR 49 per year and includes free express delivery, free returns, and early access to sales.

## Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept iDEAL, Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and Klarna (pay in 30 days or in 3 instalments). Bank transfers are accepted for business orders only.

## Do you offer student discounts?
Yes. Verified students receive 10% off NovaTech-branded laptops and tablets. Student discounts cannot be combined with other promotions.

## Can I change my delivery address after ordering?
Yes, as long as the order is still "processing". Once shipped, the address can no longer be changed.

## Do you have physical stores?
We have two stores: Amsterdam (Kalverstraat) and Rotterdam (Lijnbaan). Both are open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00.

## How do I contact Customer Support?
By chat on the website, by email at support@novatech.example, or by phone at +31 20 555 0100, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 20:00 and Saturday 09:00 to 17:00.

## Do you price match?
NovaTech matches the price of authorised Dutch retailers for identical, in-stock products within 14 days of purchase. Marketplace sellers and flash sales are excluded.
