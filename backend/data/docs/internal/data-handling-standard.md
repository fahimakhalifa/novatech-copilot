# Customer Data Handling Standard

## Classification
Customer email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, and payment details are classified as Confidential.
Order history is classified as Internal.

## Who May Access Customer Data
Only Customer Support agents and administrators may look up customer records, and only to resolve an active customer request.
Customers may only see their own orders and account details.

## Disclosure Rules
Never read out or share a customer's full email address, phone number, or home address in chat or email. Confirm identity with the order number and postcode instead.
Payment card numbers are never stored by NovaTech and must never be requested from customers in chat.

## Incident Reporting
Any suspected data leak must be reported to security@novatech.example within 1 hour of discovery.

## Retention
Order records are kept for 7 years for tax purposes. Support chat transcripts are deleted after 12 months.
