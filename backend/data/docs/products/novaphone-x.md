# NovaPhone X

## Overview
The NovaPhone X is NovaTech's flagship smartphone, launched in June 2026. It is designed for users who want a premium camera and all-day battery life in a compact 6.1-inch body.
Current pricing and stock are available in the NovaTech store.

## Key Specifications
- Display: 6.1-inch OLED, 120 Hz, 2,000 nits peak brightness
- Processor: Nova N3 octa-core chip
- Memory: 8 GB RAM
- Storage: 128 GB or 256 GB
- Cameras: 50 MP main, 12 MP ultra-wide, 10 MP 3x telephoto; 12 MP front camera
- Battery: 4,500 mAh, 45 W wired charging (0 to 70% in 30 minutes), 15 W wireless charging
- Water resistance: IP68 (up to 1.5 m for 30 minutes)
- Colours: Midnight Black, Glacier White, Forest Green

## Software Support
The NovaPhone X receives 5 years of operating system updates and 6 years of security updates.

## In the Box
Phone, USB-C to USB-C cable, SIM tool. A charger is not included.

## Compatibility
Works with the SoundWave ANC headphones and NovaBook laptops for NovaLink file sharing and clipboard sync.
