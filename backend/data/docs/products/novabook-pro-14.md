# NovaBook Pro 14

## Overview
The NovaBook Pro 14 is a lightweight laptop for professionals and students, weighing 1.35 kg.
Current pricing and stock are available in the NovaTech store.

## Key Specifications
- Display: 14-inch 2.8K (2880 x 1800) IPS, 90 Hz
- Processor: Intel Core Ultra 7
- Memory: 16 GB or 32 GB LPDDR5X
- Storage: 512 GB or 1 TB NVMe SSD
- Battery: 72 Wh, up to 15 hours of video playback
- Ports: 2x Thunderbolt 4, 1x USB-A, HDMI 2.1, headphone jack
- Weight: 1.35 kg

## Software
Ships with Windows 11 Pro. Includes a 1-year licence for NovaSync backup software.
