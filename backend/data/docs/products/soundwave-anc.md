# SoundWave ANC Headphones

## Overview
Over-ear wireless headphones with adaptive active noise cancellation, built for travel and long working days.
Current pricing and stock are available in the NovaTech store.

## Key Specifications
- Battery life: up to 40 hours with ANC on, 55 hours with ANC off
- Fast charge: 10 minutes of charging gives 5 hours of playback
- Bluetooth 5.4 with multipoint (connect two devices at once)
- Codecs: SBC, AAC, LDAC
- Weight: 250 g
- Foldable design with a hard carry case

## Noise Cancellation Modes
Adaptive ANC, Transparency mode, and Off. Modes can be switched with the touch panel on the right ear cup or in the NovaTech app.

## Care
The headphones are not water resistant. Clean the ear cushions with a dry cloth; replacement cushions are sold separately.
