# Vacation and Paid Time Off Policy

## Annual Entitlement
All full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year, in addition to public holidays.
Part-time employees receive vacation pro rata to their contracted hours.
Employees with 5 or more years of continuous service receive 2 additional vacation days per year (27 days total).

## Accrual
Vacation accrues monthly at 1/12 of the annual entitlement. New employees can take accrued days after completing their first month of employment.

## Requesting Time Off
Vacation must be requested in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences of 3 days or more.
Requests for 1-2 days need 2 working days' notice.
Managers must approve or decline requests within 3 working days.
During the peak season blackout (1 November to 31 December), Customer Support and Warehouse staff may take at most 5 consecutive vacation days, and only with director approval.

## Carry-Over
Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire.
Unused days beyond the carry-over limit are not paid out, except on termination of employment.

## Sick Leave
Sick leave is separate from vacation. Employees receive up to 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within the first 12 months after birth or adoption.
