# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility
Office-based roles (Engineering, Marketing, Finance, People Operations) follow a hybrid model: at least 2 days per week in the Amsterdam office, on Tuesdays and Thursdays as anchor days.
Warehouse and Retail staff are on-site roles and are not eligible for remote work.
Customer Support agents may work fully remotely after completing their 3-month probation period.

## Working From Abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year, with manager approval. Requests must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance so Legal can check tax and data-protection requirements.

## Equipment
NovaTech provides a laptop, a monitor, and a headset for home working. Employees also receive a one-time home office allowance of EUR 400, claimable through the expense process.

## Security Requirements When Remote
Employees must connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
Customer data must never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.
Working from public Wi-Fi without the VPN is prohibited.

## Core Hours
All employees must be available between 10:00 and 15:00 CET on working days, regardless of location.
