# Travel and Expense Policy

## General Rules
Expenses must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of being incurred, with an itemised receipt attached.
Expenses without a receipt are reimbursed only up to EUR 25, and only with a written explanation.

## Approval Limits
- Up to EUR 500: approved by the direct manager.
- EUR 500 to EUR 2,000: approved by the department director.
- Above EUR 2,000: requires approval from the CFO before the expense is incurred.

## Travel
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer, with director approval.
Train travel is preferred for journeys under 4 hours within Europe.
Hotel costs are capped at EUR 180 per night in most cities and EUR 250 per night in London, Paris, and Zurich.

## Meals
The daily meal allowance during business travel is EUR 60. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Client Entertainment
Client entertainment above EUR 150 per person requires prior approval from the department director.

## Reimbursement
Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly salary run.
